About Calm Waters

Calm Waters is a peaceful fishing game designed for moments of relaxation. Take in the scenery and enjoy the soothing sounds. Upgrade your fishing rods, buy new biome to get more gold; that is, if you want to.
Created as a school project for Web Technologies.

How To Play

You can sit back and let your little fisherman catch fish for gold. Use your gold to buy rod upgrades or unlock new biomes. Check your Stats to track progress. Use the to-do list if you want to keep track of stuff to do.
That's it.
